The first issue. Most of this week went into rearranging the site, so this one is mostly about that.

## what changed

- **Archives.** [/archives](https://cptx.ee/archives/) is new: the [library](https://cptx.ee/archives/library/) holds some of the books Merzeede and I own, on shelves you can rearrange by shelf, colour, author, decade, language or size. Every book has its own page, with the posts that mention it. The books I wrote are under [written by me](https://cptx.ee/archives/written-by-me/).
- **A new menu.** Three lines: home, about, newsletter / croquis, recuerdos, photos / addons, pockets, archives. [About](https://cptx.ee/about/) gathers the manifesto, contact and the legal docs. The [manifesto](https://cptx.ee/manifesto/) has new sections on the principles behind the site and how it works.
- **Search.** [/search](https://cptx.ee/search/) looks through every croquis and recuerdo, and the library. Accents don't matter.
- **Easier reading.** Posts are now set in Literata, a typeface made for reading long texts on screens. Lists of posts are lighter, and every post ends with a line to share it.
- **Photos.** [/photos](https://cptx.ee/photos/) has a cover for each place; photos sit in rows that keep their shape, and open in a simple viewer.
- **Pockets** are back in the menu, each with its own page.
- **Readers.** The site now counts visits, reads and countries, as daily totals only: no cookies and nothing that identifies you. The numbers are public on [/addons/readers](https://cptx.ee/addons/readers/), and each post says how many times it has been read. How it works is written down in the [legal docs](https://cptx.ee/legaldocs/).
- **This newsletter.** A weekly issue by email, and every issue is kept on the [kiosk](https://cptx.ee/newsletter/kiosk/).
- **Behind the scenes.** Every change is now checked before it goes live, a broken update rolls itself back, and the code is a little tidier.

## what was written

No new croquis this week; the writing went into the site itself.
