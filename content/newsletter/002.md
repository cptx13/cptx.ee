The second issue. The furniture is mostly in place, so this week went into filling the rooms.

## what changed

- **Rimas.** Poems now have their own section, [/rimas](https://cptx.ee/rimas/), in the menu between recuerdos and photos. Croquis is prose only; old links to poems still work.
- **Two books of poems.** All 200 poems of *Poemas de un joven no leído* (2019–2021) and the 27 of *Ahora que soy adulto* (2021–2024) are now on rimas, each linking to its book. The book pages under [written by me](https://cptx.ee/archives/written-by-me/) list their poems, and *Recuerdos: Vol.1* lists its recuerdos.
- **Reads.** [/addons/reads](https://cptx.ee/addons/reads/) brings together the articles I keep (★) and the latest posts from the blogs I follow.
- **Workbench.** [/pockets/workbench](https://cptx.ee/pockets/workbench/) is now a map of my tech projects, each with its status and a guide: the iPod, a Kindle freed from Amazon, an R36S handheld, Pop!_OS on a ThinkPad, a home server on an old ThinkPad that keeps our photos, the site itself, Obsidian, and a few still waiting.
- **Bedside table.** Books on the [bedside table](https://cptx.ee/pockets/bedsidetable/) link to their page in the library, with a cover, and the book page says when it was there.
- **Readers.** The [counters](https://cptx.ee/addons/readers/) now count people, not machines: a visit counts only when someone scrolls, taps or clicks. Nothing times how long you stay. The page also shows how much of the traffic is machines.
- **Getting around.** Under every post, cards for the older and newer one in the same section, and a link back to all of it. The home page has one search field: type to filter the titles, press enter to search every text. The [archive map](https://cptx.ee/addons/map/) fits on a phone.
- **This newsletter.** The email now looks like the issue page.
- **Behind the scenes.** Security updates, and a few typos fixed in *Ahora que soy adulto*.
