## what changed

- **Films.** A new shelf, [/archives/films](https://cptx.ee/archives/films/): 114 films from our DVD binder and boxes, with posters and details. Sort them by genre, title, director, decade or colour; each one links to IMDb, Letterboxd, a trailer and where to watch it. TV series and documentaries have a shelf of their own.
- **Archives.** The [archives](https://cptx.ee/archives/) page is simpler: each collection is a heading, a total and a row of covers. The books I wrote, 287 books, 135 records, 114 films. CDs are next.
- **Library.** Refreshed from my latest scan: 287 books, *[House on the Borderland](https://cptx.ee/archives/library/9781405972949/)* among the new ones. 32 covers showed another edition and now show the one on our shelves, and every book finally has a cover, a few found in bookshops' pages or photographed at home.
- **Bedside table.** *[El mayor de mis defectos](https://cptx.ee/archives/library/9789504930600/)* by Roberto Fontanarrosa joins the [bedside table](https://cptx.ee/pockets/bedsidetable/).
- **Behind the scenes.** Security updates and a typo fixed in a recuerdo.
