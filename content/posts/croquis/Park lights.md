A misty night throughout the Baltic shore  
trees are dancing and leaves are blown  
passersby and their playful dogs  
all together under a light-filled cold.  

Trashcans rattle, dark crows on top  
one more breeze and it's now four  
the times I've shivered on my way home.  

A misty night throughout the Baltic shore  
three kids are running and leaves are blown  
the park is quiet and covered with fog  
I no longer see the paths of stone.  

Cars honking, some men shout  
one more beep, and it's now four  
the times I've startled on my way home.
