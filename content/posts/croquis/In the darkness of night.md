In the darkness of night  
I will try to write once more  
one of those untold stories  
about the thoughts that never roam  
the ones that stay well sat  
in the stables of my farm.  

Every now and then I get back to my self  
I play around with those timid thoughts  
the ones that barely go away  
the ones that in days like today  
are craving to run around this lettered head.  

I wonder if this short improvised poem  
decorated with metaphors of inconclusive thoughts  
will ever see the light of day  
or if it shall become a well behaved idea  
from the stables of a head named farm.
