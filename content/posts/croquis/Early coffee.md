A busy street compliments and complements a sunny morning. Rushers passing by do not seem preoccupied with their routines. The occasional seagull cry does not distract the early goers, yet somehow, the more focused a walker is, the more distracted they get. Only those unbothered by the weight of the watch hand or the blinking time table for departures seem to be aware of their surroundings. Couple kids, a mother with a stroller and a young weimaraner pup seem to be the only ones who have noticed the seagull in search for the sunnier spot. Autumn is here, the streets get busier and Vana-Kalamaja regains the paused yet hectic Baltic living I seem to love. In a busy Kalamaja street the sun dresses the routine of those who can afford to not rush, and for a lucky Spaniard observing all, a gift of early morning.
