A weekly digest by email is on its way: what changed on the site, what was written, what is being written, and a few other things worth passing on. Every issue will also be kept on the site, so nothing is only in your inbox.

This page explains how it will work before the sign-up form appears.

## What is collected

Your **email address**, and nothing else. No name is needed.

## Signing up

You enter your address in a form on this site. You then get one email asking you to confirm. Nothing is sent until you do (this is called double opt-in).

## Who sends it

Emails are sent by [Buttondown](https://buttondown.com), a small newsletter service. Your address is stored with Buttondown for the sole purpose of sending this newsletter. Buttondown is based in the United States, so your address is stored there. Its own [privacy policy](https://buttondown.com/legal/privacy) applies to how it keeps that data.

## No tracking in the emails

Open tracking and link tracking are switched off: emails contain no tracking pixels, and links go straight to where they say.

## Leaving

Every email has a one-click unsubscribe link. Unsubscribing stops all emails immediately. You can also write to [hello@cptx.ee](mailto:hello@cptx.ee) to have your address deleted completely.

## Never

Your address is never sold, shared or used for anything other than this newsletter.
