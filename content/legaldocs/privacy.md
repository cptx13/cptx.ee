cptx.ee is a personal website run by me, Carlos (cptx). This page explains what happens to information about you when you read it. It is written to be read, not to impress a lawyer.

## The short version

- No ads, no trackers, no analytics scripts, no social media widgets.
- No cookies. See [cookies](/legaldocs/cookies/).
- The site itself does not log your IP address or keep records of your visits.
- Nothing on a page loads from another company's servers: fonts, images and scripts all come from cptx.ee.
- If you subscribe to the newsletter, your email address is kept only for sending it. See [newsletter](/legaldocs/newsletter/).

## Who is responsible

I am, as the person who runs the site. Write to [hello@cptx.ee](mailto:hello@cptx.ee) about anything on this page.

## What happens when you open a page

Your browser asks for a page; the site sends it. To deliver it, two parties see your request:

- **The hosting provider, [exe.dev](https://exe.dev).** cptx.ee runs on a small virtual server rented from exe.dev, which handles the secure (HTTPS) connection and passes requests on to the site. Like any host, it sees technical details such as your IP address while doing so. How exe.dev handles that is described in [its privacy notice](https://exe.dev/docs/privacy-notice).
- **The site itself.** It reads your request to send the right page and then forgets it. The site's own program does not write access logs, does not store your IP address and does not identify you.

## What is not collected

No name, no email (unless you subscribe), no account, no location, no device fingerprint, no reading history.

## Visit counts

Not yet. Public counters (visits, reads, readers' countries) are planned. When they arrive, this page will explain exactly how they work before they start counting. The plan: only daily totals are kept, nothing that could point back to a person.

## Links to other sites

Posts and pockets link to other websites, and the contact page links to my profiles elsewhere. Once you follow a link you are on their site and under their rules. The cheatsheet's reading list is fetched by the server, so reading it here does not contact those sites.

## Your rights

If you live in the EU or the UK you have the right to ask what personal data I hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, and to object to its use. For readers who have not subscribed there is nothing to hand over. Subscribers can unsubscribe with one click from any email, or ask me to delete their address. You can also complain to your data protection authority.

## Changes

When something changes (for example when visit counts or the newsletter go live), this page is updated and its date changes. The history of every change is public in the site's [source code](https://github.com/cptx13/cptx.ee/commits/main/content/legaldocs).
