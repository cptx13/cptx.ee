cptx.ee is a personal website run by me, Carlos (cptx). This page explains what happens to information about you when you read it. It is written to be read, not to impress a lawyer.

## The short version

- No ads, no trackers, no analytics scripts, no social media widgets.
- No cookies. See [cookies](/legaldocs/cookies/).
- The site itself does not log your IP address or keep records of your visits; it only keeps daily totals (see [counting readers](#counting-readers)).
- Nothing on a page loads from another company's servers: fonts, images and scripts all come from cptx.ee. The only exception is the newsletter form, which sends your address to Buttondown when you press subscribe.
- If you subscribe to the newsletter, your email address is kept only for sending it. See [the newsletter](#newsletter).

## Who is responsible

I am, as the person who runs the site. Write to [hello@cptx.ee](mailto:hello@cptx.ee) about anything on this page.

## What happens when you open a page

Your browser asks for a page; the site sends it. To deliver it, two parties see your request:

- **The hosting provider, [exe.dev](https://exe.dev).** cptx.ee runs on a small virtual server rented from exe.dev, which handles the secure (HTTPS) connection and passes requests on to the site. Like any host, it sees technical details such as your IP address while doing so. How exe.dev handles that is described in [its privacy notice](https://exe.dev/docs/privacy-notice).
- **The site itself.** It reads your request to send the right page and then forgets it. The site's own program does not write access logs, does not store your IP address and does not identify you.

## What is not collected

No name, no email (unless you subscribe), no account, no location, no device fingerprint, no reading history.

## Counting readers

The site keeps simple public counts, shown on the [readers](/addons/readers/) page and as "read N times" under each post and newsletter issue. They are made on this server, without cookies, scripts from other companies or anything stored in your browser:

- **Only people count.** A tiny script tells the server "someone is here" (sending only the page's address) the first time you scroll, tap, click or type on a page. It has no timer: nothing measures how long you stay or how far you read. Programs that just copy pages never send it, so they don't show up in the counts.
- **Visits and reads:** when that happens, the total for that page and day goes up by one (once per page per visitor per day). A visit to a post is what "read N times" counts.
- **Visitors:** to count how many different people came on a given day, the server mixes your IP address and browser name with a random value that exists only in memory and is replaced every midnight, and keeps the result in memory for that day only. It is never written down, and after midnight it can't be linked to anything.
- **Countries:** your IP address is looked up in a local country list ([DB-IP](https://db-ip.com), a file on the server, so no one else is asked) and only the country is added to the day's total.

What is stored is only daily totals per page, per post and per country, plus two plain numbers per day: how many times pages were loaded and by how many IP-and-browser pairs, automated programs included. The [readers](/addons/readers/) page shows them next to the people count, so you can see how much of the traffic is machines. No IP address, no identifier, no history of who read what. Bots and link previews are left out. If JavaScript is off, you only add to those two plain numbers.

## The newsletter

The newsletter is everything sent to subscribers by email. Most weeks that is an **issue**: what changed on the site, what was written, what I wrote on the same days in earlier years, and what I have been reading, listening to and watching. Now and then it may be a short note about the site that is not an issue. Every issue is also kept on the site, on the [kiosk](/newsletter/kiosk/), so nothing is only in your inbox; notes that are not issues are not kept.

- **What is collected:** your email address, and nothing else. No name is needed.
- **Signing up:** you enter your address in the form on [/newsletter](/newsletter/). Each issue comes in English and Spanish in the same email. You then get one email asking you to confirm, and nothing is sent until you do (double opt-in).
- **Who sends it:** [Buttondown](https://buttondown.com), a small newsletter service. Your address is stored with Buttondown, in the United States, only to send this newsletter. When you press subscribe, your browser sends the form straight to Buttondown, which also sees your IP address and uses it to keep out spam sign-ups. Its own [privacy policy](https://buttondown.com/legal/privacy) applies to how it keeps that data.
- **No tracking in the emails:** open and link tracking are switched off. Emails contain no tracking pixels, and links go straight to where they say.
- **Leaving:** every email has a one-click unsubscribe link, which stops all emails at once. You can also write to [hello@cptx.ee](mailto:hello@cptx.ee) to have your address deleted completely.
- **Never:** your address is never sold, shared or used for anything other than this newsletter.

## Links to other sites

Posts and pockets link to other websites, and the contact page links to my profiles elsewhere. Once you follow a link you are on their site and under their rules. The cheatsheet's reading list is fetched by the server, so reading it here does not contact those sites.

## Your rights

If you live in the EU or the UK you have the right to ask what personal data I hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, and to object to its use. For readers who have not subscribed there is nothing to hand over. Subscribers can unsubscribe with one click from any email, or ask me to delete their address. You can also complain to your data protection authority.
