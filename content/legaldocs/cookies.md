cptx.ee does not set cookies: no tracking cookies, no advertising cookies, no "functional" cookies. That is why there is no cookie banner.

## The one thing kept in your browser

If you press the **◐** button to switch between light and dark, your choice is saved in your browser's local storage under the name `theme`, so the next page opens the way you left it. It contains only the word `light` or `dark`, it never leaves your device, and it is not sent to the site. If you never press the button, nothing is saved and the site simply follows your system's light or dark setting.

To remove it, clear this site's data in your browser settings.

## Third parties

No page loads anything from another company, so no other company can set cookies through cptx.ee.

## If this changes

Planned visit counts will also work without cookies. Should cookies ever be needed, this page will say so first, and anything not strictly necessary would ask you before being set.
