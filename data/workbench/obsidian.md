## how it's set up

- One vault, Mathom, kept small: notes only. Heavy files live in a folder next to it.
- Obsidian Sync keeps it on the phone.
- A "to publish" folder is the outbox: what I write there on the phone ends up on this site.
- Articles I clip with the Web Clipper become the [reads](/addons/reads/) I keep.
