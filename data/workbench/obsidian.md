## guide

1. **One vault, Mathom, kept small**: notes only. Heavy files live in a folder next to it, so Sync keeps working.
2. **Obsidian Sync** keeps it on the phone.
3. **A "to publish" folder is the outbox**: what I write there on the phone ends up on this site.
4. **Articles I clip** with the Web Clipper become the [reads](/addons/reads/) I keep.
